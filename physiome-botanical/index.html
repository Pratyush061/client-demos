Our Services

Comprehensive care for your recovery

We provide tailored treatments to help you overcome pain and achieve optimal movement.

Physiotherapy

Expert assessment and diagnosis of musculoskeletal pain, focusing on finding the root cause of your discomfort.

Sports Injury Rehab

Customised rehabilitation programmes and data-driven athletic movement analysis to get you back in the game safely.

Clinical Massage

Targeted sports and relaxing Swedish massage to relieve tension, improve circulation, and aid recovery.

Acupuncture

AACP certified acupuncture treatments to help manage pain and stimulate the body's natural healing processes.

Joint Taping

Professional taping techniques to support joints, reduce pain, and improve proprioception during movement.

Specialist Injections

Corticosteroid and hyaluronic acid injections, including comprehensive assessment and a structured treatment plan.

Your Practitioner

Meet Ali Momeni Pour

Ali is a Senior Physiotherapist at Nuffield Health and an NHS First Contact Practitioner (FCP). With a BSc in Physiotherapy from Keele University and postgraduate FCP training from St George's University, he brings a wealth of clinical expertise to every session.

As the Rehabilitator for Hackney Rugby Football Club, he has extensive experience in sports injuries. He holds advanced training in musculoskeletal medicine, spinal manual therapy (SOMM), and is certified in sports massage, taping, and acupuncture (AACP).

He is highly skilled in orthopaedic triage, imaging referrals, and specialist referrals, ensuring you receive the precise care you need.
