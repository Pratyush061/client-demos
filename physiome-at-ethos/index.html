About Your Practitioner

Ali Momeni Pour

Senior Physiotherapist at Nuffield Health & NHS First Contact Practitioner (FCP)

Ali holds a BSc in Physiotherapy from Keele University and postgraduate FCP training from St George's University. As the Rehabilitator for Hackney Rugby Football Club, he has extensive experience in sports injury management.

  • Advanced training in musculoskeletal medicine and spinal manual therapy (SOMM)
  • Sports massage, taping and acupuncture (AACP)
  • Orthopaedic triage, imaging and specialist referrals
