Dental Implants

Replacing missing teeth with permanent, natural-looking results.

At the Guy Favero Ferber Dental Clinic, our implant specialists utilize advanced technology to offer precise, minimally invasive implant placement. Whether you are missing a single tooth or require a full-mouth rehabilitation, we provide a permanent solution designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Our Implant Treatments

  • Single Implants: The ideal replacement for a single missing tooth, blending seamlessly with your natural smile.
  • All-on-4 / Full-Mouth Implants: Complete arch restoration utilizing four or more strategically placed implants.
  • Bone Grafting & Sinus Grafts: Advanced surgical procedures to rebuild bone density and ensure a secure foundation for your implants.

Precision Technology

We leverage Nobel Biocare guided surgery, X-guide navigation, and high-resolution CBCT 3D scanning. This ensures optimal implant positioning, predictable outcomes, and a faster recovery.
