Frequently Asked Questions
Do you accept new patients?

Yes, we are currently welcoming new patients for consultations, hygiene, and specialist treatments. Please contact our reception to arrange your initial visit.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, from 9:00am to 6:00pm. We are closed on weekends.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

While we welcome referrals from other dental practices, you do not need a referral to book a consultation directly with any of our specialists.
