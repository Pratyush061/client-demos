Aesthetic Dentistry

Veneers & Smile Makeovers

Bespoke aesthetic restorations designed to harmonize with your features.

A beautiful smile can dramatically improve your confidence. Our cosmetic dentists specialize in designing smiles that are striking yet incredibly natural. We combine artistic flair with meticulous planning to deliver results tailored precisely to your facial symmetry and personal goals.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are ultra-thin, custom-made layers of porcelain bonded to the front of your teeth. They are an elegant solution for correcting discolouration, closing gaps, and improving the shape of worn or chipped teeth.

Comprehensive Smile Design

A smile makeover may involve a single procedure or a combination of treatments, including:

  • Porcelain veneers or composite bonding
  • Professional home teeth whitening
  • Orthodontic alignment
  • Gum contouring to correct a "gummy" smile

Using our 3Shape digital intra-oral scanner, we can take highly accurate digital impressions and provide a preview of your new smile before any treatment begins.
