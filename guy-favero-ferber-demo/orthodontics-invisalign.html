Orthodontics & Invisalign

Discreet alignment solutions for adults and children led by our Consultant Orthodontist.

Orthodontic treatment goes beyond aesthetics; properly aligned teeth are easier to clean and function more efficiently. Led by Dr Francesca Pantanali, Specialist Orthodontist, our clinic offers a comprehensive range of modern, discreet braces for patients of all ages.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign uses a series of virtually invisible, custom-made aligners to gradually move your teeth into the desired position. They are removable, meaning you can eat, drink, and brush normally during your treatment.

Advanced Orthodontic Options

  • Lingual Braces: Attached to the back (lingual) side of the teeth, these are completely hidden from view, making them a popular choice for adult professionals.
  • Ceramic Braces: Fixed braces featuring tooth-coloured or clear brackets that blend in with your natural teeth.
  • Metal Braces: Traditional, highly effective fixed braces, often the preferred choice for complex child and adolescent cases.
