Specialist Care

Root Canal / Endodontics

Expert care dedicated to saving your natural teeth and relieving pain.

When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed—often due to deep decay, repeated dental procedures, or a crack in the tooth—specialist endodontic treatment is required. Our goal is to relieve your pain, remove the infection, and save your natural tooth.

Expert Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment (endodontics) is a highly skilled procedure. Our endodontists, Dr Marina Dettori and Specialist Endodontist Dr Wilfred Chan, possess advanced training to handle complex cases, including re-treatments of previously failed root canals.

Advanced Diagnostics

We utilise cutting-edge CBCT 3D scanning to gain a comprehensive view of the tooth’s complex root canal anatomy. Combined with the use of high-magnification dental microscopes, this allows our specialists to achieve unparalleled precision, ensuring the highest possible success rate for the treatment.
